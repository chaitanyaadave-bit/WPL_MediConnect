# Mediconnect

Traditional healthcare services often require patients to visit clinics in person for doctor appointments, medicines, diagnostic reports, bills and payments, which is time-consuming and inconvenient. MediConnect is a single online platform where registered patients can search for doctors, book appointments, order medicines, book health checkup packages, view their reports and bills, and make payments.

Patients register and receive a unique Member ID, which they use to access these services. They can search for doctors by specialty, view doctor profiles and book appointments online. The portal covers eight specialties: General Physician, Dermatology, Dental Care, Pediatric Care, Diabetology, Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation, Psychology / Therapy and General Allergy Clinic.
